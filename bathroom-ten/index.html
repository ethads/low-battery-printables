How to clean a really grim bathroom in ten minutes when you have no energy

A ten-minute bathroom clean in five small jobs, with the spray doing the hard work, for when low energy has let things get grim.

You've been avoiding it. The sink has a film, the loo needs attention, the shower has a mood, and the towels have been on the floor for longer than you'd like to say. You use the bathroom every day, so you know exactly what it looks like. That's part of why it feels bad.

Bathroom cleaning is one of those jobs that looks huge from the doorway and turns out to be small when you start. The trick is to remove the choices and let the spray do the work. Ten minutes, five jobs, in order, and you can stop at any point.

What you're not doing

You're not doing grout, limescale on the taps, or the inside of the cabinet. You're doing the things you can see. A bathroom that's wiped and rinsed and has a clean towel is a bathroom you can use without wincing, and that is the goal.

Gather first (one minute)

Pulling things out from under the sink is the step where people lose momentum, so do it first, all at once:

  • Any bathroom or multi-surface spray. Any brand is fine.
  • Two cloths, or a roll of kitchen paper. One for the sink, one for the loo.
  • A bin bag.
  • One clean towel, set aside for the end.

Open the window. It clears the smell faster and it makes you feel better immediately.

The five jobs

Do them in this order. The order matters, because it lets the spray sit while you do the other jobs.

  1. Bin and clear the edges (1 minute). Empty bottles, old razors, hair from the plug, loo roll tubes, anything that's obviously rubbish. Straight in the bag.
  2. Spray sink, loo and shower (1 minute). Spray them all, then leave it. Don't wipe yet. The spray is working.
  3. Towels and mat to the wash (1 minute). Everything on the floor, everything damp, into the pile by the machine.
  4. Wipe: sink, then loo, then shower (5 minutes). Always in that order, and never use the loo cloth on the sink. Rinse the shower with water. The loo seat, rim and handle count.
  5. Floor: wipe or sweep the middle only (1 minute). The edges can wait.

Then put out the fresh towel. Use the bathroom. That's the reward, and it's real.

Short on energy?

If you only have the energy for one job, do the loo. It makes the biggest difference for the least effort. Spray it, leave it, count to a hundred, wipe it. That's a win and you're allowed to stop there.

A few safety notes worth keeping in mind:

  • Never mix cleaning products. Use one spray at a time, with a window open.
  • Out of spray? Hot water and a drop of washing-up liquid works fine for most things.
  • If there's mould or limescale that won't shift, it's not your fault. Note it down, read the label on a stronger product, and do it another day.

If the shower curtain is the grim thing, many can go in the machine on a cool wash with a towel. Check the label.

Why it works

The sprayed surfaces do most of the cleaning while you do the rest, so the wiping is quick. And the jobs are so small that none of them gives you time to talk yourself out of it. If you notice the thought "I can't be bothered", the answer is the same every time: just do the next line.

What counts as done

Done is the loo wiped, the sink wiped, the shower rinsed, and a fresh towel out. It's okay if it isn't spotless. It's okay if it took twelve minutes, or if you stopped after job four. A bathroom you can walk into tomorrow and not feel bad about is done.
